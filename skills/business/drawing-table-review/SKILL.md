---
name: drawing-table-review
description: Review extracted tables and drawing regions where headers, dimensions, units, part revisions or image quality affect an answer.
allowed-tools: [mcp__openviking__read_knowledge_source]
---

Inspect the relevant source region with its title, headers, units, drawing number and revision. Keep row and column associations, merged cells and footnotes when interpreting a table.

Separate extracted text from visual interpretation. A blurry digit or conflicting dimension remains unresolved; do not average candidate readings or replace the original text with a model guess. Cite the original page and region, not only a generated summary.

Use published, validated parser results. If a region or required page is missing, identify that gap. A photograph alone does not establish a hidden material property or manufacturing tolerance.
